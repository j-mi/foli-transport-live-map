Föli live map · architecture notes

How data moves

Three kinds of data take three different paths. Live bus positions go from Föli through the backend’s memory to the map every 5 seconds and are never stored. Timetable data is cached in memory. Only your favourites and a copy of the stops live in the database.

Who talks to whom

Live buses

One request to Föli every 5 s, however many browsers are open. The newest snapshot sits in memory and is pushed to every open map over Server-Sent Events.

Timetable (GTFS)

Loaded at start and re-checked every 6 h. Served from memory. When a new dataset appears, the stops are copied into PostGIS for “stops near here”.

Your data

Favourites and nearby searches are SQL against PostGIS. Departures are fetched from Föli when you open a stop and shared by everyone for 15 s.

When the app decides data is missing or old

Every layer has a fixed limit. When one is reached, the status bar changes. The 20-second rule is the safety net: it is measured from fetched_at, the moment the backend last got fresh data from Föli, so it catches every kind of failure, including ones that take the other rules longer to name.

How one bus is tracked

Each bus is identified by Föli’s vehicleref. The backend copies it to id, and the map feature carries it as properties.id. There are no per-bus update messages, though: every 5 s the backend sends the complete list, and the map replaces all the points at once. The id matters where something has to follow one bus, which is the popup.

Not handled: one bus with an old position

The status bar’s age is for the feed as a whole, not per bus. In the recorded sample, bus 202095 (line 82) was in Föli’s 11:30 UTC feed with a position recorded at 01:16 UTC, about 10 hours earlier. Its validuntiltime (recorded time + 10 min) had long passed. The app draws it like any other bus; only its popup reveals the age. The feed includes validuntiltime for every bus, so the data to detect this case is available.

Where this lives in the code

backend/app/integrations/foli_client.py
Every call to Föli: timeouts, retries with jitter, circuit breakers
backend/app/integrations/siri_parser.py
vehicleref → id, delay sign, buses without a position skipped
backend/app/realtime/poller.py
The 5 s loop, last good snapshot, when status becomes DOWN
backend/app/realtime/broadcaster.py
One small queue per browser; slow browsers never block others
backend/app/api/vehicles.py
The SSE stream: vehicles, status and heartbeat events
frontend/src/realtime/vehicleStream.ts
20 s old-data rule, 35 s watchdog, polling fallback
frontend/src/map/layers/VehiclesLayer.tsx
Pushes each snapshot into MapLibre with setData
frontend/src/map/popups/VehiclePopup.tsx
Follows one bus by its id across snapshots
